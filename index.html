FAQ

Good to know.

Can I write an entry for a past date?

Yes. Open Calendar, select the date you want, and tap the plus button to create an entry for that day.

How many photos can I add?

You can attach up to three photos to each journal entry.

How do I find an older entry?

Use Search to find text from an entry, browse entries by month on Home, or choose a date in Calendar.

Where is my journal stored?

Your entries and selected photos are stored locally on your device. Clean Journal does not require an account.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app may also remove locally stored journal data. Make sure your device backup settings meet your needs before deleting the app.

Privacy Policy

Clean Journal is designed to keep your journal private. The app does not require you to create an account.

Your journal entries and the photos you choose to attach are stored locally on your device. Clean Journal does not upload your diary content to a developer-operated server.

The app does not sell your personal information or use your journal content for advertising.

If you contact support by email, the information you provide is used only to respond to your request.

This policy may be updated when the app changes. Any revision will be published on this page with a new effective date.
